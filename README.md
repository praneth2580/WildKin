# WILDKIN - MONSTER SANCTUARY

### Official Gameplay Rules (Draft v0.1)

## 1. Overview

Monster Sanctuary is an offline creature breeding and sanctuary management game.

Players begin with a small sanctuary, a handful of monsters, and limited resources.

The objective is to build a thriving sanctuary, create unique bloodlines through breeding, maintain healthy monster populations, and develop a legacy of creatures that can compete against other players when connected locally.

There are no predefined monsters.

Every monster is generated from genetic traits and may be unique.

---

# 2. Core Principles

The game revolves around five pillars:

### Discover

Obtain eggs and hatch new creatures.

### Raise

Feed and care for monsters.

### Breed

Combine genetics to create new generations.

### Manage

Balance sanctuary capacity, food production, and caretaker assignments.

### Compete

Battle and trade with other players through local connections.

---

# 3. Monster Lifecycle

Every monster progresses through the following stages:

```text
Egg
↓
Infant
↓
Juvenile
↓
Adult
↓
Elder (Optional Future Feature)
```

Only Adults may breed.

---

# 4. Monster Genetics

Every monster is generated from a DNA profile.

Possible DNA categories include:

* Body Type
* Eyes
* Mouth
* Horns
* Wings
* Tail
* Pattern
* Element
* Personality Traits

Example:

```text
Body: Wolf
Eyes: Star
Horns: Crystal
Element: Lightning
Personality: Curious
```

The resulting monster is unique.

---

# 5. Bloodlines

Every monster belongs to a bloodline.

Bloodlines begin with a founding monster.

Example:

```text
Stormfang Line
Generation 1
↓
Generation 2
↓
Generation 3
```

Bloodlines track:

* Descendants
* Victories
* Mutations
* Heritage Value

---

# 6. Eggs

Eggs are the primary source of new monsters.

### Early Game

Eggs are common.

### Mid Game

Eggs become less common.

### Late Game

Breeding becomes the primary source of new monsters.

Rare eggs may still be discovered through exploration.

---

# 7. Feeding

Monsters require food to maintain Trust.

Food categories may include:

* Protein Feed
* Growth Fruit
* Crystal Herbs

Some monsters prefer specific foods.

Preferred foods provide bonuses.

---

# 8. Treats

Treats influence development.

Examples:

* Spicy Berry
* Moon Fruit
* Crystal Candy

Treats may affect:

* Mutation rates
* Breeding outcomes
* Personality development
* Trait inheritance

---

# 9. Trust

Every monster has a Trust value.

Trust is affected by:

* Feeding
* Care quality
* Caretaker assignment
* Neglect

High Trust provides:

* Better breeding
* Better battle performance
* Reduced behavioral problems

---

# 10. Neglect and Behavioral Decline

If a monster is neglected it may progress through several stages.

## Stage 1: Uncooperative

Effects:

* Ignores commands
* Reduced breeding success
* Reduced training efficiency

## Stage 2: Hostile

Effects:

* Refuses activities
* Reduces sanctuary stability
* Increases caretaker workload

## Stage 3: Rampant

Effects:

* Monster becomes uncontrollable
* Cannot breed
* Cannot battle
* Cannot perform normal activities

Rampancy is considered a critical condition.

---

# 11. Rampant Monsters

Once a monster becomes Rampant:

* Release is no longer possible.
* Normal care no longer works.
* The monster must either:

  * Enter Rehabilitation
  * Be Put Down

This decision is permanent.

---

# 12. Rehabilitation Center

Unlocked later in sanctuary progression.

Rules:

* Limited capacity
* Expensive operation
* Long recovery times

Successful rehabilitation returns the monster to service.

Failure may result in permanent loss.

---

# 13. Releasing Monsters

Monsters may be released only before becoming Rampant.

Released monsters leave peacefully.

Benefits may include:

* Heritage Points
* Sanctuary Reputation
* Bloodline Legacy

Release is intended as a population management tool.

---

# 14. Population Management

Every sanctuary has a population limit.

Population is influenced by:

* Habitat capacity
* Building upgrades
* Available caretakers

Exceeding capacity increases neglect risk.

---

# 15. Caretakers

Caretakers automate sanctuary operations.

Examples:

* Keeper
* Breeder
* Trainer
* Veterinarian
* Rehabilitation Specialist

Caretakers have:

* Level
* Experience
* Specialization

---

# 16. Caretaker Affinity

Caretakers build Affinity with monsters they repeatedly care for.

Affinity unlocks:

* Better feeding decisions
* Trust bonuses
* Early behavior warnings
* Improved breeding preparation

Affinity is monster-specific.

Reassigning a caretaker reduces the usefulness of that accumulated knowledge.

---

# 17. Sanctuary Buildings

Buildings unlock new gameplay systems.

Examples:

### Nest

Egg incubation.

### Breeding Den

Breeding operations.

### Food Farm

Food production.

### Training Grounds

Battle preparation.

### Research Hut

Genetic research.

### Trading Post

Local player interactions.

### Rehabilitation Center

Rampant monster recovery.

### Heritage Hall

Bloodline records.

---

# 18. Research

Research unlocks knowledge about genetics.

Players may discover:

* Trait inheritance tendencies
* Mutation probabilities
* Food interactions
* Personality effects

Research provides information, not guarantees.

---

# 19. Breeding

Adult monsters may breed.

Offspring inherit traits from both parents.

Possible outcomes:

* Standard inheritance
* Dominant inheritance
* Recessive inheritance
* Mutation

Breeding is the primary late-game progression system.

---

# 20. Mutations

Mutations are rare genetic events.

Examples:

* Star Eyes
* Crystal Wings
* Halo Horns
* Shadow Tail

Mutations may become inheritable.

Rare mutations form the basis of elite bloodlines.

---

# 21. Expeditions

Players may send monsters and caretakers on expeditions.

Rewards may include:

* Eggs
* Food
* Relics
* Research Data
* Rare Traits

---

# 22. Relics

Relics provide passive sanctuary bonuses.

Examples:

* Reduced Trust decay
* Improved egg quality
* Improved caretaker efficiency

---

# 23. Battles

Players may connect locally and challenge one another.

Possible connection methods:

* QR Code
* Bluetooth
* Local Network

Battles are short and automated.

Battle performance is influenced by:

* Genetics
* Training
* Trust
* Personality
* Care Quality

---

# 24. Heritage Hall

The Heritage Hall records significant monsters.

Recorded information may include:

* Founders
* Champions
* Longest Bloodlines
* Rare Mutations
* Legendary Caretakers

The Heritage Hall preserves sanctuary history.

---

# 25. Victory Philosophy

There is no final ending.

Success is measured through:

* Bloodline longevity
* Sanctuary reputation
* Rare discoveries
* Genetic achievements
* Battle success
* Preservation of monster history

The ultimate goal is to create a sanctuary whose creatures, caretakers, and bloodlines tell stories that no other player can replicate.
